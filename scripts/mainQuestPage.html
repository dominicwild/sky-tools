Quests

22
 Sign in to edit
  • History
  • Purge
  • Talk (0)

Daily Quests are a set of four tasks that players can accept each day and, upon completing, are rewarded with currency such as Candle or Season Candles.

Before starting a Quest the player most accept it by interacting with the Return Shrine Statue in Home, or by visiting the Ancestors waiting at the Aviary Village Archway Cafe Counter.

Once a Quest has been completed, returning to interact with the Return Shrine Statue or the archway counter Ancestors again will reward players with one Candle or Season Candle per completed Quest.

Overview[]

Daily Quests and their associate Spirits are being tracked on the Quests/Daily Quest Spirits page.

Pressing the upper-left corner of the screen (iOS and Android), the left arrow on the D-Pad (PlayStation and Switch), or the Shift key (PC) will toggle a menu showing the Daily Quests.

The daily list of Quests will always fall under five different categories:

  • Friend Actions/Interactions.
  • Gameplay Actions.
  • Visit a Meditation Shrine.
  • Find the Light.
  • Relive a Spirit's memories.

Each day the location of the Daily Quests change, cycling through almost every Realm in turn, typically following directly after the day's chosen Daily Treasure Candle Realm. Neither Isle of Dawn nor the Eye of Eden will have Quests. During Seasonal Events, Quests are nearly identical to those given off-Season, though they will reward the player with Season Candles instead.

Some Quests can only be done in a specific area within the Realm in question. These include Meditation Quests, or Quests such as to rescue a Manta from Darkness. For these types of Quests, players can use a Map Shrine, where their locations within the Realms will be marked with yellow icons. Most of the time, all Quests for the day can be completed within the daily Realm, but there is no requirement that this should be the case.

Trivia[]

  • After completing all available quests the Archway Cafe Counter will close for the remaining day, turning off the lights and lifting up the bar stools on the counter.
  • In Patch 0.15.0 that arrived September 2021, access to Daily Quests was moved from the Quest Giver to the Return Shrine Statue at Home. In the much later Patch 0.23.5 from December 2023, Daily Quests were briefly changed to be exclusively accessed from the Return Shrine Statue at Aviary Village, before Patch 0.23.6 returned them back to Home a few days later.
  • When Patch 0.15.0 changed access to Daily Quests, a particular spirit similar to Sleepy Traveler who had been teaching the Daily Quests at the home during the Off-Season (the Daily Quest Giver), left the home. Since patch 0.26.5 the Spirit at the counter in Aviary Village is considered to be this Quest Giver.
  • The Quest to meditate at the Forest Brook Shrine was previously called "Meditate at Forest's hollow tree."
  • The Quest to meditate on the First Floor of Vault was previously called "Meditate with the Spirit Mantas."
  • For the Season of Enchantment, the "catch the light" Quests were replaced by the colored light Quests that are originally given by the Quest Giver in Forgotten Ark. After the Season ended, not only did the old light Quests return, but the colored light Quests stayed.
  • In Patch 0.27.0 that arrived October 2024, "Ride a Manta in Daylight Prairie" was changed to "Ride with a Manta".
